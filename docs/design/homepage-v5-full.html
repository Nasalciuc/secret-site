Frequently asked questions

How can I find discounted business class flights for last-minute travel?+
Finding discounted business class flights for last-minute travel can be challenging, but there are a few ways to increase your chances. Airlines often offer last-minute promotions, and consolidators like us hold unpublished inventory that never appears on booking sites. Call the concierge line — last-minute is where a human search pays off most.
What is the best way to book cheap international business class flights?+
Request a quote with flexible dates if you can. A concierge compares consolidator fares across 30 partner airlines and comes back with the options that fit your route and budget.
How much does a business class flight ticket usually cost?+
Business class flight tickets vary greatly depending on the airline, destination, and time of booking. Typically, international business class tickets range from $1,100 to $5,000. Booking in advance or during an airline sale can secure the best price.
Can I find business class flights at a discount during peak travel seasons?+
Yes — availability is tighter, but consolidator fares exist year-round. Give us your dates early and we'll hold options as they appear.
What is included in a business class ticket for international flights?+
Lie-flat or angled seats, priority check-in and boarding, lounge access, premium dining and a larger baggage allowance. Details vary by airline; your quote lists what's included.
Are business class tickets refundable or changeable?+
Most consolidator fares are changeable for a fee; refund rules depend on the fare class. Every quote states the change and refund terms before you book.
